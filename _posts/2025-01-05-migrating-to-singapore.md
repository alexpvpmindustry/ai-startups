If you're an expat family planning to enroll your child in Singapore's education system, you’ve likely come across the terms **AEIS** and **SPERS**. These are essential entrance exams that international students need to pass to gain admission to local schools in Singapore. In this post, we'll explain what these exams are, their purpose, and how **Golden Tuition Academy (GTA.sg)** can help your child succeed.

## What is the AEIS Exam?  
The **Admissions Exercise for International Students (AEIS)** is an annual centralized exam conducted by Singapore's Ministry of Education (MOE). It is designed for international students who wish to join Singapore's primary or secondary schools. The AEIS tests a student's proficiency in two core subjects: **English** and **Mathematics**. These subjects are crucial as they form the foundation of Singapore's rigorous academic curriculum.

Students typically take the AEIS in September or October, and successful candidates are admitted to schools in January of the following academic year. The exam is highly competitive, and students are assessed on their ability to adapt to the local syllabus.

### Key Details of the AEIS Exam:
- **Who Can Take It:** International students aged 7 to 16 who are not currently enrolled in a local school.  
- **Subjects Covered:** English (focus on grammar, comprehension, and writing) and Mathematics (problem-solving and critical thinking).  
- **Purpose:** To place students in the appropriate academic level within the Singapore education system.  

## What is the SPERS Exam?  
The **Supplementary Placement Exercise for Returning Singaporeans (SPERS)** is another placement exercise but is specifically aimed at Singaporean students returning from overseas. However, some provisions allow international students who missed the AEIS to take SPERS, depending on eligibility. Similar to AEIS, SPERS evaluates a student’s English and Mathematics skills to determine the most suitable school and academic level.

SPERS typically takes place later in the year, around November or December, providing a secondary pathway for students who missed the AEIS deadline.

### Key Details of the SPERS Exam:
- **Who Can Take It:** Returning Singaporean students and some international students.  
- **Subjects Covered:** English and Mathematics, similar to AEIS.  
- **Purpose:** To facilitate seamless integration into the Singapore education system for returning or late applicants.

## Why Are AEIS and SPERS Important?  
Both AEIS and SPERS serve as gateways to Singapore's public schools, known for their high academic standards and emphasis on holistic education. Admission through these exams offers international students an opportunity to experience Singapore's world-class education and access resources that pave the way for future success.

However, these exams are known for their challenging nature, as they are aligned with Singapore's competitive curriculum. Many students find the level of English and Mathematics significantly advanced compared to what they are used to in other countries.
